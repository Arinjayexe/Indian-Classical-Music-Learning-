Indian Musical Instruments

Discover the fascinating instruments that bring Indian classical music to life through melody and rhythm.

The Art of Musical Instruments

Indian classical music uses a wide variety of traditional instruments. These instruments create melody, rhythm, accompaniment and unique musical expressions. They are commonly grouped into string, percussion and wind instruments, among other categories.

String Instruments

🎸

Sitar

The sitar is a plucked string instrument widely associated with Hindustani classical music. It has a distinctive resonant sound and is known for melodic improvisation.

Plucked Strings
🎻

Veena

The veena is a traditional Indian string instrument strongly associated with Carnatic music. It is known for its expressive melodic sound.

Plucked Strings
🎶

Sarangi

The sarangi is a bowed string instrument known for its expressive tone and ability to imitate vocal-like musical expressions.

Bowed Strings

Percussion Instruments

🥁

Tabla

The tabla is a pair of drums used in many Indian musical performances. It produces a variety of rhythmic sounds and patterns.

Percussion
🥁

Mridangam

The mridangam is a double-sided drum and one of the principal percussion instruments in Carnatic classical music.

Percussion
🔔

Ghatam

The ghatam is a clay pot percussion instrument used in Carnatic music. It creates rhythmic patterns through striking its surface.

Percussion

Wind Instruments

🪈

Bansuri

The bansuri is a bamboo flute that produces a soft and melodious sound. It is used in both classical and folk music.

Wind Instrument
🎺

Shehnai

The shehnai is a traditional wind instrument often associated with celebrations, ceremonies and classical performances.

Wind Instrument
🎵

Nadaswaram

The nadaswaram is a powerful reed instrument popular in South Indian temple music and traditional celebrations.

Wind Instrument

Did You Know?

Indian musical instruments are not just used for entertainment. Many are deeply connected with festivals, ceremonies, traditions, storytelling and cultural identity.
