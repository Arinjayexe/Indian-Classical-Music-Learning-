Start with the Basics

Learning Indian classical music requires patience, practice and listening. Beginners can start with musical notes, basic rhythm, simple exercises and gradually explore ragas and compositions.

Your Learning Path

01

Understand Swaras

Learn the seven basic musical notes and their role in creating melodies.

02

Practice Rhythm

Explore tala, rhythmic cycles, beats and simple clapping exercises.

03

Explore Ragas

Understand the structure, melodic patterns and expressions of different ragas.

04

Listen and Perform

Listen to musical performances and practise simple compositions regularly.

A Simple Daily Practice Routine

Warm-Up

Spend a few minutes practising basic swaras slowly.

Note Practice

Repeat simple note sequences with attention and clarity.

Rhythm Practice

Clap along with simple rhythmic patterns.

Listening

Listen carefully to classical music and notice its melodies.

Test Your Musical Knowledge

Answer these three questions to test your understanding.

1. How many basic swaras are there?

2. Which instrument is a bamboo flute?

3. What is a raga mainly associated with?

Music is a Journey

Every note you learn and every rhythm you practise brings you closer to understanding the beauty of music. Enjoy the process of learning and exploring.
