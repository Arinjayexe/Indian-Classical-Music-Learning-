Melody, mood and musical expression

Discover the World of Ragas

Explore the melodies of Indian classical music and discover how ragas create unique musical moods and expressions.

What is a Raga?

A raga is a melodic framework in Indian classical music. It uses a particular arrangement of musical notes, melodic movements and characteristic phrases to create a distinct musical identity and mood. Ragas are not simply scales; they have their own rules, expressions and patterns.

Elements of a Raga

🎼 Swaras

The seven basic musical notes are Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha and Ni. They form the foundation of melody.

⬆️ Aaroh

Aaroh describes the ascending movement of notes in a raga.

⬇️ Avaroh

Avaroh describes the descending movement of notes in a raga.

💫 Mood

Different ragas can express peacefulness, devotion, joy, longing and many other emotions.

Popular Indian Classical Ragas

Raga Yaman

🌙 Evening

Raga Yaman is known for its graceful and peaceful character. It is a popular introductory raga for students.

Tradition: Hindustani

Raga Bhairav

🌅 Morning

Raga Bhairav has a serious, meditative and devotional character, often associated with the early morning.

Tradition: Hindustani

Raga Malkauns

🌌 Late Night

Malkauns is known for its deep, contemplative and expressive character.

Tradition: Hindustani

Raga Bhimpalasi

☀️ Afternoon

Bhimpalasi is often associated with longing, expressive melodies and a reflective mood.

Tradition: Hindustani

Raga Kalyani

🎵 Flexible Performance

Kalyani is a prominent Carnatic raga known for its rich melodic possibilities and bright character.

Tradition: Carnatic

Raga Mohanam

✨ Expressive Melody

Mohanam is a pentatonic raga with a beautiful, accessible melodic structure.

Tradition: Carnatic
